A boutique of two

Meet Divya
& Vithika.

Two friends, two perspectives — one quiet obsession with weddings done right. Since 2010, our small Bangalore studio has hand-crafted under a hundred weddings — every one of them attended by us, planned by us, and carried, very gently, on our shoulders from first phone call to the morning after.

We work in pairs because weddings happen in pairs. One of us is always at your side. We don't outsource the heart of it.

How we work

Three phases.
One steady pair of hands.

From first phone call to the morning after, every wedding moves through the same simple rhythm — two of us, every step.

01

Discover

A slow opening conversation. Your story, your families, the wedding you've been quietly imagining. We send a 15-minute questionnaire, and then we just listen.

  • First call & questionnaire
  • Vision & family conversation
  • Budget shaping, no pressure
  • Date & venue scouting
02

Design

Moodboards, fabric swatches, vendor curation. We hand-pick every florist, every photographer, every caterer — none of them outsourced, all of them ones we've worked with before.

  • Mood & design boards
  • Vendor curation & contracts
  • Décor, stationery, logistics
  • Run-of-show, minute by minute
03

Deliver

The two of us, on-site, every function. Divya at the bridal entry. Vithika at the door of your parents' room with chai. We don't hand it off — we live it with you.

  • Both founders, on-ground
  • Family & guest management
  • Vendor command, hour-by-hour
  • The morning-after wrap
